# Alveo privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-16

Alveo is a free app developed by Zeke Zappa Developments (iezappa). This policy explains, in plain
language, what happens to what you write in it.

## What the app stores

- Everything you write or record in Alveo — check-ins, journal entries,
  session notes, thought records, medications, your safety plan — is stored
  **only on your device**.
- It **never leaves your device** and is **not stored on any server**: not the
  developer's, and not the server that delivers the web version.
- The developer **cannot see, recover or delete** your data.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

## Sensitive data

What you record about your mental health is **sensitive data** under
Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Ley 25.326, article 2) and a special
category of data under the GDPR (article 9). Because it never leaves your
device, the developer never receives or processes it.

## Storage on your device

The app uses your device's or browser's storage (a local database and, on the
web, `localStorage`, IndexedDB, OPFS and the service worker cache) **only to
work**: to keep your data and settings and to open without a connection. It
uses no tracking cookies.

Nothing is encrypted at rest. The optional PIN keeps casual eyes out of the
app; on the web version it is only a deterrent and does not protect against
someone with access to your browser or device.

## Exports are files you control

Backups (JSON, CSV), the Obsidian export, session PDFs and daily Markdown notes
are **unencrypted files**. The app warns you before writing each one. Once
saved, where they go — a USB drive, a cloud folder, an email — is your choice
and outside this app: keep them somewhere private.

## Internet connections

The app makes no network requests of its own. It only opens what you tap: a
phone number from the crisis lines, a donation page, or the contact page, in
your phone app or browser.

## Your rights and how to delete your data

- **Delete everything:** Settings → Your data → **Delete all my data**. Your
  data is also deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the site's data in
  your browser.
- **Get a copy:** Settings → Your data → **Export**.
- Since the developer does not have your data, they cannot hand it over or
  delete it for you.

## Children

Alveo is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the change is mentioned
in the app's release notes.
